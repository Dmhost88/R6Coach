# Scoreline

The match score: your side first in `team-us`, theirs in `team-them`, in `score` mono with tabular figures.

- Markup: `.rc-score` holding `.rc-score__us`, `.rc-score__dash` (an en dash), `.rc-score__them`, and an optional `.rc-score__meta` for round and side.
- Always "us – them" in that order, whichever side you're on.
- Don't animate the numbers or flash the score on a round win; the coach says it.
